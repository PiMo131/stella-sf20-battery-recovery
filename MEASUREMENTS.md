## Measurements still worth collecting

- Exact battery label/model and capacity
- Voltage of each series group after normal full charge
- Voltage of each group after resting
- Voltage of each group near low state of charge
- Full-charge total voltage
- Recovered usable capacity (Wh/Ah)
- Temperature rise during charge/discharge
- BMS output voltage switch OFF/ON
- Unknown pin voltages switch OFF/ON

> Do not infer cell balance from total pack voltage alone.
